# Patch 040 — Direct Morphology Integration

Install over 039. In Morphology, choose **Morphology engine → Legacy / Direct Section**. Legacy is the default for existing configurations. The choice is persisted per mod in `thicc.json` as `morphology_engine`. Preview and Apply use the same engine; both evaluate the original backup buffers. Restore is unchanged. Changing the engine invalidates the Belly live baseline key. The previous Legacy/Continuous setting still controls the original regional field interpolation.

Direct Section adds an anatomical hip field in model space with a quintic vertical envelope. The lower bound is unchanged from experiment 74 (`knee + .12*(hip_z-knee)`), the upper bound is waist, and the gain peak now equals the effective crotch landmark. The final silhouette maximum may differ because of original width, radial masks and combined regional fields. Strength is the provisional experiment-74 ratio .14265 times the anatomical hip half-width per slider unit. Active Skin contour samples (401 heights, per-side widths) provide the section scale; invalid coverage fails explicitly. The same spatial field applies to all eligible vertices. No iterative Hc calibration or post-smoothing is required. Glutes/Thighs/Calves retain original regional formulas without Hc coupling; Belly and optional post-processing continue through the existing production pipeline. Coupled Hip-Thigh and Relative Silhouette are Legacy-only; the controls are disabled in Direct Section, but their saved values remain available upon switching back. Directional expansion remains supported. Legacy shading derivatives sample the new field at arbitrary coordinates, while other shading options use the original export pipeline.

Experiment 74 remains the historical read-only lab. Legacy-specific hip monitors and root-cause helpers explicitly select Legacy to preserve diagnostic meaning. Current parameter overlays include the selected engine.

Validation: 95 unit tests passed. On a disposable copy of real Suoming (422324 vertices, 527941 triangles), Legacy Hips 2 matched the earlier baseline exactly. Direct Section Hips 2 had zero reported inverted triangles. Direct Section Hips 5 + Glutes .67 + Thighs 1.13 + Calves .82 had zero reported inverted triangles or midline crossings, unchanged height, and identical float32 Preview/Apply positions; original Position/Vector backups unchanged. Windows GUI, gameplay animation, alternate shading modes and other mods require further validation. The new engine is opt-in; production Legacy remains available.
